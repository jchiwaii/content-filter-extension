02 / HOW IT WORKS

Make it yours.

  1. 01

    Add to Chrome

    Install Safe Browse from the Chrome Web Store. Pin it to keep the controls close by.

  2. 02

    Pick your filters

    Choose what to filter, add your own words, or allow a site you already trust.

  3. 03

    Browse as usual

    New comments and feed updates get checked as they appear. Pause from the popup anytime.

03 / PRIVACY

Your tabs stay yours.

Safe Browse checks the page in your browser. It doesn't send the words you read or the pages you visit to a filtering server.
