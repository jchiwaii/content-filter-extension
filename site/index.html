What Safe Browse does.

Cleaner words
and links

Read the conversation without all the profanity around it.

  • Removes matching words from pages
  • Filters unwanted links in posts
  • Checks new comments as they appear

Fewer explicit images.
Fewer adult pages.

Helps keep content you weren’t looking for off your screen.

  • Blurs images with explicit labels or links
  • Blocks known adult websites
  • Lets you block other sites yourself

Safer searches.
Your own rules.

Choose what gets filtered and what you want to leave alone.

  • Keeps supported searches in safe mode
  • Lets you add your own filtered words
  • Allows trusted sites and pauses anytime

03 / PRIVACY

Your tabs stay yours.

Safe Browse checks the page in your browser. It doesn't send the words you read or the pages you visit to a filtering server.
